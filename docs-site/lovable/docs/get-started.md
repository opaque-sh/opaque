> [!WARNING]
> **Planned flow**
>
> The Opaque app is not live. This page describes how it is designed to work. Nothing here can be done yet.

## What you need

- A wallet on Robinhood Chain with a little ETH for network fees.
- Some $OPA to shield.

## The flow

1. **Connect your wallet.** Your keys for private notes are meant to come from one wallet signature, so the same wallet gives you the same keys on any device. This depends on a wallet-key binding that is not in the circuit yet. See [Keys](/docs/keys).
2. **Shield.** Choose an amount. You approve the pool first, then shield. The deposit is public. You receive a private note.
3. **Hold.** Your notes show up in the app, decrypted on your device. Private $OPA notes earn from fees.
4. **Send or exit.** Send a note to another Opaque user, or exit to any address. An exit is public.

## Before you shield

- Read [Staying private](/docs/staying-private). Early on, the pool is small, and a small pool hides you less.
- Deposits are capped, and the cap rises on a fixed schedule. See [Shield and unshield](/docs/shield-and-unshield).
