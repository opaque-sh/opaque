title: Get $OPA
description: Where $OPA trades and how to move it into the pool.
---

$OPA is an ordinary ERC-20 on Robinhood Chain, launched on the Pons launchpad. Anyone can hold, send and trade it.

> [!NOTE]
> **Contract address**
>
> Announced at launch. Do not trust any address before then.

## Buy it anywhere

- On its Pons bonding curve.
- On a Uniswap v4 pool once it graduates from the curve.
- Through aggregators, because it is a plain ERC-20.

Buying through these routes is a normal public purchase. The tokens sit in your wallet, in public, and do not earn.

## Shield what you hold

Open the app and shield $OPA. It moves into the pool as a private note, where it starts earning. The shield is public. See [Shield and unshield](/docs/shield-and-unshield).

## Buy it privately

> [!WARNING]
> **Planned**
>
> Paying with ETH in the app and receiving $OPA straight into a private note needs exit targets, which are not built yet. See [Integrations](/docs/integrations).

Until then, the route is to buy publicly and then shield.
