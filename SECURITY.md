## Bounty

A public bounty is planned. It will start small while the pool is small and scale with vault size. Terms will be published here and on opaque.sh before any mainnet deployment.

## Scope notes

Because the contracts are designed to be immutable, a bug cannot be patched after launch. The only remedy is a voluntary migration, one note at a time, through an exit target. This is why the circuit and the vault get the most review.
